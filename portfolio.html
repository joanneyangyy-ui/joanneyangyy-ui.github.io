Where GenAI fit in

AI built the automation. It was not the automation.

  • Pair-building with AI assistantsI used AI assistants to draft and debug Power Query M, DAX measures, Excel formulas and flow expressions, then verified every output against source reports before it went to a GM.
  • An agent experiment that failed usefullyI first tried an AI coding agent to download the daily revenue reports. It kept stalling on sign-in, connectivity and token limits, so I rebuilt the job as a deterministic Power Automate Desktop flow. Repetitive, credentialed work belongs in a predictable robot.
  • Copilot as the user's helperThe department agenda workbook ships with plain-English Copilot prompts, so property managers can apply settings without touching formulas.
  • A written memory of gotchasEach build left a cheat sheet of failure modes (date columns Power Query cannot trust, capacity maths that drifts 30x). That document is what keeps AI help accurate on the next change.

Project 01 · Rooms revenue management

Revenue Scorecards from the RMS

Power Automate DesktopPower Query MDAXPower BI REST APIOneDrive

Problem

Revenue managers exported two RMS reports per property by hand every morning, then rebuilt the same one-page scorecard for 10 general managers, none of whom had Power BI licences.

What I built

A desktop robot that downloads all 20 exports, a Power Query pipeline that reshapes them into a star schema, 80+ DAX measures, and a cloud flow that refreshes the model and exports a PDF per property.

Outcome

About 30 minutes saved each day on the RM report. Fully automated up to the PDF; I documented the licensing cost of automating the final email and recommended keeping that step manual.

Problems I solved along the way

  • The export's date column is an uncached formula.Power Query rebuilds every stay date from the report title and row position instead.
  • Occupancy maths drifted by 30x.Monthly figures need capacity × days in month; daily figures need plain capacity. Separate measures for each grain fixed it.
  • Derived room counts were unstable.Rooms ÷ occupancy on a quiet night produced 2,500% occupancy. Capacity is now a maintained reference table.
  • Last-year lookups ignored my filters.REMOVEFILTERS on the date table clears the relationship before shifting a year back.

Project 02 · Human resources

An HRIS Built from Scratch

Power Automate cloud flowsAzure SQLMicrosoft FormsSharePointApprovalsTeams adaptive cardsWord templates

Problem

Hiring, payroll setup and reviews ran on email chains and a legacy payroll system. Forms went missing between property HR, corporate payroll and managers.

What I built

An HRIS on an Azure SQL database, with history migrated from the legacy payroll system, and a chain of flows that moves each employee from offer to 90-day review with no manual hand-offs.

Outcome

Every hire carries one tracker ID from approval to review. Documents file themselves into the right property folder, and the right accountant or manager is told the moment they need to act.

Project 03 · Food and beverage

Dynamic Game-Night Reservations

Microsoft BookingsPower AutomateExcel Online tablesExpressions

Problem

Leadership wanted guaranteed dining before arena events, operations wanted no manual follow-up, and the old static form could not stop a seating from being overbooked.

What I built

A Bookings page with 90-minute seatings capped per event date. A flow parses each appointment into a slot key, event date and arrival time, then rewrites that slot's rows in the restaurant's live tracker.

Outcome

Guests book themselves, full seatings close automatically, and the floor team works from one up-to-date list with party size and notes.

Project 04 · Guest experience

Silent Shop Review Dashboard

Microsoft FormsPower QueryPower BIWeighted scoring model

Problem

Mystery-shopper visits to three restaurant concepts came back as free-text forms. Directors could not compare locations or see which part of the visit lost points.

What I built

A 140-point weighted model across Ambiance (35), Service (80) and Food & Beverage (25), three dashboard versions, drill-down to question level, and a feedback wall for written comments.

Outcome

Weekly per-location PDFs go to the director of restaurant services and each GM. The question-level view turns "service was slow" into a specific coaching point.

Sales, budgets, promos, voids and online reviews for five restaurant and retail brands sat in separate POS and GL systems. Directors saw results monthly, after the chance to act had passed.

A 21-page weekly Power BI report that ranks every outlet against budget and last year, then drills into food and bar mix, promos, voids by manager and reason, buffet sales, top sellers, reviews, net income and average spend.

Project 06 · Recognition

Quarterly Performance Celebration

Power BIDAX ranking with eligibility gatesGL actuals vs budgetGuest survey scores

Problem

Quarterly awards across hotels, restaurants, banquets and service departments were picked from spreadsheets, and nobody could explain why one team won.

What I built

One dashboard with a rule per category: first an eligibility gate (budget met, survey threshold met), then a ranking metric. If no one passes the gate, the card says "No one eligible" instead of crowning a loser.

Outcome

Eleven categories decided by the same transparent logic every quarter, filterable by brand and property, with the supporting charts one click away.

Project 07 · Payroll and labour cost

Wage Cost Automation

Excel VBA macrosPower QueryTime-and-attendance exportsRole and department mappingPivotTables

Problem

Wage cost only reaches the general ledger once a month. To track labour daily, each hotel accountant rebuilt wage cost by hand from payroll exports, about two hours every day.

What I built

A macro-enabled workbook per property. The accountant enters the month and entity code; Power Query pulls the time-and-attendance export, maps every role to a department, prices the hours and refreshes every dashboard tab.

Outcome

Two hours a day saved for each of 15 hotel accountants, roughly 30 hours a day across the team. The output also feeds the weekly department head agenda.
