Loan Approval Evaluator

A loan-screening tool that takes an applicant's income, employment, credit history and loan details and returns an approve or deny decision, with debt-to-income, loan-to-income and payment-to-income ratios checked against thresholds.

0.984AUC on the team's held-out test set
92.6%test accuracy
3models compared: logistic regression, random forest, gradient boosting

Logistic-regression marginal effects explain the top drivers of each decision; permutation importance shows debt-to-income and credit score matter most. A leakage check confirms the result does not depend on interest rate.

Approach

Start from the decision, not the dashboard.

01 · Frame

Name the decision

Who acts on this number, how often, and what would they do differently? That sets the metric, the grain and the cadence.

02 · Build

Fix the foundation

Reconcile sources to the ledger first. A brand metric nobody trusts is worse than no metric.

03 · Measure

Model what moves it

Forecasts, drivers and scenario tools that show cause, not just change, with the uncertainty stated.

04 · Land

Tell it, then automate it

One page leaders read in two minutes, delivered on a schedule, with the controls that let it run without me.
